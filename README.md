## Dmytro Plavutskyi

Founder of **Ryntra** — one app for onchain markets, built around a person's
own wallet: it prepares every action and shows it before you sign, your wallet
signs it, and the chain proves it.

**Launch once. The market keeps working.**

### [Ryntra on Solana](https://ryntra.io)

Launch a token in one signature, see every market launched on Ryntra with its
health, trade Solana tokens and tokenized stocks from $1, and buy by your own
rule — on the web and as an Android app in the Solana dApp Store.
