## Dmytro Plavutskyi

Founder of **Ryntra** and **Ryntra Guard** — two money products built around a
person's own wallet: the product prepares every action and shows it before you
sign, your wallet signs it, and the chain proves it.

### [Ryntra](https://ryntra.io) — tokenized stocks on Solana

Invest in tokenized stocks from $1, in your own wallet, on Solana. Buy a piece
of a company in dollars, or set Autoinvest by your own rule.

[**ryntra.io**](https://ryntra.io) ·
[Open-source kit — `ryntra-solana-evidence`](https://github.com/ryntra-io/ryntra-solana-evidence) ·
[Try it in 30 seconds](https://github.com/ryntra-io/ryntra-solana-evidence#try-it-in-30-seconds)

### [Ryntra Guard](https://arc.ryntra.io) — stablecoin hub on Arc

Your stablecoin hub on Arc, for you and your team: get paid, pay, top up, watch
and prove every payment, from your own wallets.

[**arc.ryntra.io**](https://arc.ryntra.io) ·
